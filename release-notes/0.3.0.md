# Doole's Core & Crucible 0.3.0

A Minecraft mod that turns gear progression into a long climb. Start with wood, work up through **nine tiers** and upgrade the *same* gear at the smithing table, keeping its name, enchantments, trim and wear.

**Minecraft 1.21.1, 26.2 and 26.3 · Fabric and NeoForge**

---

## The tier ladder

**Wood → Bone / Flint → Copper → Iron → Emerald → Diamond → Obsidian → Netherite → Reinforced Netherite**

- **Wood, bone, flint and copper** are crafted at the crafting table. Bone and flint replace stone tools, which can't be crafted.
- **From iron up, everything is a smithing-table upgrade** of the tier below. Iron and diamond gear can't be crafted directly, and vanilla's diamond-to-netherite upgrade is replaced by the chain.
- **Obsidian, netherite and reinforced gear** float in lava and don't burn.

## Gear for every tier
- **Tools and weapons:** pickaxe, axe, shovel, hoe, sword, spear and mace.
- **Ranged and utility:** bow, crossbow, fishing rod and shield. Shears come in bone, flint, copper and vanilla iron.
- **Armour** from copper up, plus **horse armour** and **wolf armour** from copper to reinforced.
- **Bows and crossbows** hit harder and their arrows pierce more armour at higher tiers. **Fishing rods** and **shears** also scale with tier.
- **Shields** raise faster, recover faster after an axe hit and resist knockback while blocking. The top tiers also partly cover you from the side.
- **Hoes** till a single block, then a 3-wide row from iron, then 3×3 from obsidian. Iron hoes and up replant crops, and every tier above wood gives bonus harvest (up to +50%).

## Smithing upgrades
Put three things in the smithing table: the **upgrade template** for the new tier, your **item from the tier below**, and that tier's **alloy ingot**. The top three tiers also need a **catalyst**, which goes in an extra slot that appears when the item needs one:

| Upgrading to | Catalyst |
|---|---|
| Obsidian | Ghast Tear |
| Netherite | Wither Skeleton Skull |
| Reinforced | Nether Star |

- **Alloy ingots** (iron to reinforced) are made at the smithing table from the tier's template, the previous tier's ingot and a raw material: iron, emerald, diamond, crying obsidian, netherite, then an obsidian alloy for reinforced.
- **Upgrade templates:** the Iron template is found in chests across the world, including dungeons, villages, mineshafts, strongholds, temples, outposts, mansions, shipwrecks and ruined portals. Every later template is crafted from the one before, and any of them can be copied with netherrack, the same way as vanilla's netherite template.

### Vein Resonance
Hold **Left Alt** while mining and scroll to pick a shape. A selector under the crosshair shows your shape, and purple motes mark every block about to break.

| Level | Shapes |
|---|---|
| I | **Seam** (the whole connected vein or tree), **Bore** (1-wide hole) |
| II | adds **Facet** (3×3 face) |
| III | adds **Gallery** (3×3 tunnel), **Drift** (walkable 1×2 tunnel), **Rise** (stairs up), **Descent** (stairs down) |

Reach is 4 / 8 / 12 blocks by level. Better tools break more at once, up to 48 blocks with reinforced netherite. It always stops before your tool breaks.

## World generation
Vanilla's small ore blobs are replaced by larger **vanilla-style ore veins** with their own filler rock. They're more common in each ore's favoured biome, like coal in forests and iron in dripstone caves.

## Help in-game
- **Hold Shift** over any gear to see its stats, what tier comes next and exactly what the upgrade costs.
- A **smithing-table recipe book**, plus crafting recipes that unlock in the normal recipe book as you pick up their ingredients.
- An **advancements tab** (press L) that walks you through the whole chain.
- **Creative:** the mod has its own tab with every item and every enchanted book, and its items also appear in the vanilla Tools, Combat and Ingredients tabs in tier order.
- **JEI (optional):** a smithing category for every upgrade and alloy, info pages for alloys, templates and catalysts, and a full explanation of each enchantment on its books.

## Configuration
`config/dooles_core_crucible.json` sets tool, armour, bow, fishing rod, shears and shield stats, ore vein sizes and how often templates appear in loot. Recipes and tags can also be changed with a datapack.

## New in 0.3.0
- **Vein Resonance rework:** a new look (crosshair selector and purple motes), renamed shapes, a **Left Alt** default key (was `` ` ``, which clashed with FTB Ultimine), reach that grows with level, and a lower top-tier limit (Reinforced 64 → 48).
- **Creative tabs:** mod items now also appear in the vanilla Tools, Combat and Ingredients tabs, and the mod's tab lists every enchanted book.
- **JEI:** each mod enchantment's books have an info page.
- **Molten Tread:** walking on magma no longer hurts you. It used to give a Fire Resistance potion effect instead.
- **Gravitic Anchor:** now also stops upward launches from mace smashes and Warden sonic booms.
- **Geode Cracker:** no longer multiplies gems spilled from a broken chest or other container (a duplication exploit).

> **Upgrading from 0.2.0:** existing worlds and gear carry over. If you used Vein Resonance, the key is now **Left Alt**.
