# Doole's Core & Crucible 0.3.1

A small update: armour trims now show properly on the mod's armour, and smithing upgrades now fully repair your gear.

**Minecraft 1.21.1, 26.2 and 26.3 · Fabric and NeoForge**

For everything the mod does, see the [0.3.0 release notes](0.3.0.md).

---

## New in 0.3.1

### Armour trims on the item icon
The mod's armour (**Emerald**, **Obsidian** and **Reinforced Netherite**, plus **Copper** on 1.21.1) now shows its trim on the item icon in your inventory, the same way vanilla armour does. Every vanilla trim material works, including resin on 26.x. Trims on armour you're wearing already showed before this update.

### Darker trims on matching armour
A trim that matches its armour's colour now uses a darker shade so it still stands out, like vanilla's iron trim on iron armour:

| Armour | Trim | Shade |
|---|---|---|
| Emerald | Emerald | New darker emerald |
| Reinforced Netherite | Netherite | Vanilla's darker netherite |
| Copper (1.21.1) | Copper | Vanilla's darker copper |

This works both on the item icon and on armour you're wearing.

### Upgrades fully repair
Upgrading gear at the smithing table now gives you the new tier at **full durability**. It used to carry over how worn the old piece was. Name, enchantments, lore and armour trim still carry over as before.

> **Upgrading from 0.3.0:** existing worlds and gear carry over, and gear you already upgraded keeps its current durability.
>
> **Datapacks and resource packs:** on 1.21.1 and 26.2 the mod replaces vanilla's `copper` (1.21.1 only), `emerald` and `netherite` trim materials to add the darker shades. The vanilla values are unchanged. A datapack that also replaces those files will override the darker shades on those versions. 26.3 doesn't need this.
