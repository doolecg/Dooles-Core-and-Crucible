# Changelog

## 0.2.0
- Nine tiers from Wood to Reinforced for tools, spears, maces, armor, bows, crossbows, fishing rods and shears
- Alloy ingots, smithing upgrades that keep name, enchantments and scaled damage, and a catalyst slot for the top tiers
- Upgrade templates: Iron from structure loot, each later one crafted from the one below
- 15 enchantments ported from Applied-Enchantments
- Shift tooltip with stats and the next upgrade, smithing recipe book, JEI category, advancement tab
- Vanilla-style ore veins replace vanilla ore blobs
- Tiered shields (Wood to Reinforced Netherite): faster raise, shorter axe disable, knockback resistance while blocking and partial cover from the side at the top tiers
- Tiered horse armor and wolf armor (Copper to Reinforced Netherite)
- Balance config, `config/dooles_core_crucible.json`: durability, speeds, armor, launcher/rod/shears/shield stats, ore vein sizes and template loot chances
