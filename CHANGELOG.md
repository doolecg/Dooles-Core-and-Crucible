# Changelog

## 0.3.0
- Mod items now also appear in vanilla's Tools, Combat and Ingredients creative tabs, in tier order next to their vanilla neighbours
- The mod's creative tab now lists an enchanted book for every level of every mod enchantment
- JEI: each mod enchantment's books have an info page explaining what it does, what it goes on and its max level
- Vein Resonance: new look, with a shape selector under the crosshair and purple motes on every block that will break, instead of a list menu and a merged outline
- Vein Resonance: shapes renamed to Seam, Bore, Facet, Gallery, Drift, Rise and Descent
- Vein Resonance: default key changed from ` to Left Alt, so it no longer clashes with FTB Ultimine
- Vein Resonance: reach now grows with level (4 / 8 / 12 blocks; it was always 16) and the top-tier block limit is lower (Reinforced 64 to 48, tapering above Iron)
- Molten Tread: magma no longer hurts the wearer directly, instead of granting a Fire Resistance potion effect
- Gravitic Anchor: also stops upward launches from mace smashes and Warden sonic booms
- Fixed Geode Cracker multiplying gems spilled from a broken chest or other container

## 0.2.0
- Nine tiers from Wood to Reinforced for tools, spears, maces, armor, bows, crossbows, fishing rods and shears
- Alloy ingots, smithing upgrades that keep name, enchantments and scaled damage, and a catalyst slot for the top tiers
- Upgrade templates: Iron from structure loot, each later one crafted from the one below
- 15 enchantments ported from Applied-Enchantments
- Shift tooltip with stats and the next upgrade, smithing recipe book, JEI category, advancement tab
- Vanilla-style ore veins replace vanilla ore blobs
- Tiered shields (Wood to Reinforced Netherite): faster raise, shorter axe disable, knockback resistance while blocking and partial cover from the side at the top tiers
- Tiered horse armor and wolf armor (Copper to Reinforced Netherite)
- Balance config, `config/dooles_core_crucible.json`: durability, speeds, armor, launcher/rod/shears/shield stats, ore vein sizes and template loot chances
